## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the aspirational balance between clinical skincare authority and accessible luxury cosmetic shopping. Tailored for conscious beauty consumers, salon professionals, and retail operators, it replaces intimidating clinical starkness and tacky discount e-commerce aesthetics with a luminous, curated, and deeply serene atmosphere.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Luminous Editorial:** Ample ivory breathing space, crisp typographic contrast, and restrained metallic rose accents that mimic warm daylight reflecting off fine glass containers.
- **Accessible Luxury:** Elevated sensory touchpoints through soft, rounded rectangular contours, tactile micro-elevations, and gossamer-fine borders.
- **Operational Clarity:** High-stakes retail details—such as warehouse branch availability, SKU identifiers, and dynamic inventory levels—are conveyed through crisp, unobtrusive badges and tabular elegance without cluttering the shopping experience.

## Colors

The color system operates on high-purity ivory backgrounds and muted cosmetic pigment tones. It anchors primary brand interactions in warm metallic rose gold (`#B76E79`) and grounds critical actions, checkout CTAs, and typographic hierarchy in deep velvety burgundy (`#6A1A24`).

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#B76E79`):** Rose gold accent used for key active states, selection pills, subtle hover glows, and luxury accents.
- **Secondary (`#6A1A24`):** Deep burgundy providing authoritative contrast for primary transactional buttons, critical navigation nodes, and bold editorial headers.
- **Named Foundationals:**
  - `surface-ivory` (`#FAF8F5`): Canvas baseline, providing warmth superior to standard `#FFFFFF`.
  - `surface-clean` (`#F4F3F0`): Ultra-clean light gray/sand for container nesting, inactive panels, and table row striping.
  - `nude-soft` (`#E8D8CF`): Subtle dividers, tag fills, and input borders.
  - `rose-dust` (`#D8A49B`): Secondary badges, hover overlays, and muted illustrative highlights.
  - `charcoal-ink` (`#1E1A1D`): Deep muted black for body typography and high-legibility iconography.
- **Inventory & Branch Status Indicators:**
  - `status-in-stock` (`#2E6B4F`): Muted emerald for abundant warehouse availability.
  - `status-low-stock` (`#B46522`): Warm ochre for scarce branch stock alerts.
  - `status-out-of-stock` (`#9E2A2B`): Crimson for depletion notifications and transactional validation warnings.

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve a clean, editorial look with gentle geometry. Its crisp curves mirror modern cosmetic packaging, remaining legible in micro-scale warehouse inventory matrices and editorial product spreads alike.

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Editorial Displays & Headlines:** Set with tight tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) to maintain poise and luxury authority in lookbooks and hero banners.
- **Body & Product Descriptions:** Maintained at standard optical heights with loose leading (`1.5` to `1.75`) for smooth reading of ingredient decks, formulas, and usage guidelines.
- **Metadata, Badges & Labels:** Rendered in semi-bold and bold weights with expanded letter spacing (`0.03em` to `0.08em`) and uppercase styling for inventory stock locations, SKU tags, and category chips.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a 12-column responsive fluid grid with strict content max-widths to preserve an airy, boutique catalog feel.

### Breakpoints & Columns
- **Desktop (≥1280px):** 12 columns, `margin-desktop` (3rem), `gutter-lg` (2rem). Maximum content bounding box is capped at 1440px to prevent visual sprawl in product grids.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, `margin` (2rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Filter panels transition from sidebars to slide-out drawers.
- **Mobile (≤767px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1rem), `gutter-sm` (1rem). Product cards switch to 2-column grids or horizontal carousel formats.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical cadence relies on multiples of `0.5rem` (`8px`). Dense administrative interfaces (warehouse stock management, orders tables) employ `space-xs` and `space-sm` for cell padding, while public e-commerce showcases utilize `space-xl` and `space-2xl` to isolate featured skincare formulas and lookbook photography.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids heavy drop shadows, instead using soft, ambient depth that mirrors polished glass and luminous skin surfaces.

### Tonal Stratification & Borders
- **Base Canvas:** `surface-ivory` (`#FAF8F5`) acts as the warm unprinted sheet.
- **Elevated Cards & Sheets:** Backgrounds switch to `#FFFFFF` coupled with a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(232, 216, 207, 0.6)` (`nude-soft`).
- **Interactive Floating Layers (Modals, Slide-over Drawers, Popovers):** Use high-dispersion, warm-tinted ambient shadows:
  - *Resting Card Elevation:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(106, 26, 36, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 26, 29, 0.02)`
  - *Hover Elevation:* `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(106, 26, 36, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(183, 110, 121, 0.06)`
  - *Modal & Dropdown Elevation:* `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(30, 26, 29, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(232, 216, 207, 0.5)`
- **Glassmorphic Quick-Views & Navigation:** Sticky headers and quick-add overlays utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses gentle, organic geometry (`roundedness: 2`, standard radius `0.5rem` / `8px`).

### Corner Geometry
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Input fields, product listing cards, alert banners, and table container housings.
- **Large Panels & Modals (`1rem` / `16px`):** Slide-over checkout panels, shopping cart sheets, and brand presentation banners.
- **Fully Rounded Pills (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for branch inventory tags (`Sede Central: 12 uds`), promotion badges, filter chips, and primary conversion buttons. This contrast between structural cards and rounded pills keeps operational metadata legible and distinct.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Transactional):** Filled with secondary brand color (`#6A1A24`), label in white `label-md`, rounded pill shape (`9999px`), padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover shifts background to `#53141C` with soft ambient bloom.
- **Secondary (Rose Gold Accent):** Filled with primary color (`#B76E79`), text in `#FFFFFF`. Hover brightens slightly with a warm shadow.
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Border `1px solid #B76E79`, color `#6A1A24`, transparent background. On hover, background transitions to `rgba(183, 110, 121, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost:** Borderless, deep charcoal color, text underlined only on hover.

### Badges & Branch Stock Indicators
- **In-Stock (Sede):** Pill container with background `rgba(46, 107, 79, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(46, 107, 79, 0.25)`, text `status-in-stock` (`#2E6B4F`), font `label-micro`. Displays a subtle 6px green indicator dot alongside branch name and unit count (e.g., `● Sede Norte: 8 uds`).
- **Low-Stock Alert:** Pill container with background `rgba(180, 101, 34, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(180, 101, 34, 0.25)`, text `status-low-stock` (`#B46522`).
- **Out of Stock / Transfer Only:** Pill container with background `rgba(158, 42, 43, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(158, 42, 43, 0.25)`, text `status-out-of-stock` (`#9E2A2B`).

### Product Cards
- Contained in crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces with `0.5rem` border radius, bordered by `1px solid rgba(232, 216, 207, 0.6)`.
- Image aspect ratio 1:1 or 4:5 on neutral warm-clean backdrop (`#F4F3F0`) with subtle zoom on hover (scale `1.03`, transition duration `300ms ease-out`).
- Category label in `label-sm` uppercase, brand title in `title-md`, price in semi-bold `headline-sm`.
- Nested branch stock micro-pill list anchored near card footer, allowing customers to preview warehouse availability before entering the product detail page.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8D8CF`, border-radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Focus state replaces the border with `#B76E79` and applies an outer ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(183, 110, 121, 0.15)`.
- Error state enforces `#9E2A2B` border with clear contextual micro-copy beneath the input.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Inactive: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8D8CF`, text `#1E1A1D`, font `label-sm`.
- Active / Selected: Background `#6A1A24`, border `1px solid #6A1A24`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- Counter pill nested inside active chips rendered in `#B76E79` pill for visual density.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 18px dimensions, border `1.5px solid #D8A49B`, background `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `4px` (checkbox) or `50%` (radio).
- Checked state fills with `#6A1A24` and displays a crisp white check or center disc.

### Administrative Inventory Table
- Header row: Clean minimal surface (`#F4F3F0`), text in `label-sm` uppercase tracking `0.05em`.
- Data rows: `#FFFFFF` resting background with hover highlight in `rgba(232, 216, 207, 0.2)`. Border divider `1px solid #F4F3F0`.
- Inline quick-steppers for quantity adjustment featuring compact rose gold pill buttons.
